# Memberships and prices

## Membership options
Tidewater Fitness has three memberships. Flex is $18 a week with no minimum term and gives access to the gym floor from 6am to 9pm. Standard is $22 a week on a 12-month term and includes 24/7 access and all group classes. Premium is $29 a week on a 12-month term and adds two guest passes a month, a monthly body composition scan and 10% off personal training.

## Joining fee
There is a one-off $49 joining fee, which covers your access tag and an induction session with a trainer. The joining fee is waived for students with a valid student ID and during our January and July promotions.

## Student and senior discounts
Students and people aged 65 or over get 20% off Standard and Premium memberships. Bring proof of eligibility to reception when you sign up. The discount cannot be combined with corporate rates.

## Corporate memberships
Companies with five or more staff joining can get a corporate rate of $19 a week on Standard. Ask your HR team to email corporate@tidewaterfitness.example to set it up.

## Payment
Memberships are paid weekly by direct debit, every Monday. If a payment fails we try again on Thursday and add a $5 dishonour fee. After two failed payments your access tag is paused until the balance is cleared.
