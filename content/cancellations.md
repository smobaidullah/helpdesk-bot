# Cancelling, freezing and transferring

## Cancelling a Flex membership
Flex has no minimum term. Cancel any time at reception or through the app with 14 days notice, and your last debit will be the one inside that notice period.

## Cancelling during a 12-month term
Standard and Premium are 12-month terms. To leave early there is a $150 early exit fee, plus 14 days notice. After the 12 months are up the membership rolls onto a no-term weekly plan at the same price and can be cancelled with 14 days notice and no fee.

## Moving away or medical reasons
If you move more than 30 km from any Tidewater club, or a doctor certifies you cannot exercise for the rest of your term, we waive the early exit fee. Bring proof of your new address or a medical certificate to reception.

## Freezing your membership
You can freeze a Standard or Premium membership for between 1 and 12 weeks per year. Freezes cost $3 a week, and your term end date moves back by the length of the freeze. Medical freezes with a doctor's note are free. Request a freeze at least 3 days before you want it to start.

## Transferring a membership
You can transfer the rest of your 12-month term to a friend or family member for a $30 admin fee. They need to come in with you to sign the transfer form and get their own access tag.
