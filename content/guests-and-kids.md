# Guests, kids and visitors

## Guest passes
Premium members get two free guest passes each month. Anyone else can bring a guest for $15 a visit. Guests must be 16 or older, show photo ID and sign a waiver at reception, so guests can only visit during staffed hours.

## Casual visits
A casual day pass is $20 and can be bought at reception or in the app. A 10-visit concession card is $160 and lasts 6 months.

## Age limits
Members need to be 16 or older. Teenagers aged 14 and 15 can join with a parent or guardian's consent and must complete an induction, and they can only train during staffed hours.

## Creche
We have a creche for children from 6 months to 6 years, open Monday to Friday 8:30am to 12pm. It costs $5 per child per session, up to 90 minutes, and must be booked in the app.
