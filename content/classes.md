# Group classes

## Which classes are included
All group classes are included in Standard and Premium memberships. Flex members can book classes for $12 each. We run spin, HIIT, yoga, pilates, boxing fitness and strength classes, with around 45 classes a week.

## Booking a class
Book classes in the Tidewater app up to 7 days ahead. Bookings open at 6am each day. If a class is full you can join the waitlist and you will get a notification if a spot opens up.

## Late cancellations and no-shows
Please cancel at least 2 hours before the class starts. If you cancel late or do not show up three times in 30 days, you will not be able to book classes for 7 days.

## Beginners
Every class can be adjusted for beginners, so let the instructor know it is your first time. Yoga Foundations on Tuesday at 6pm and Strength Basics on Saturday at 9am are designed for people who are new to training.
