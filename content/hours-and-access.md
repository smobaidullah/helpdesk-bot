# Opening hours and access

## Staffed hours
Reception is staffed Monday to Friday 6am to 8pm, Saturday 7am to 4pm and Sunday 8am to 2pm. Public holiday hours are posted in the app a week ahead.

## 24/7 access
Standard and Premium members can use the gym 24 hours a day, 7 days a week with their access tag. Outside staffed hours, entry is through the side door on Harbour Lane, and the gym is monitored by CCTV with duress alarms in the changing rooms and on the gym floor.

## Lost access tags
A replacement access tag costs $15. Report a lost tag in the app straight away so we can deactivate it.

## Parking
There are 40 free car parks behind the building, limited to 2 hours between 6am and 6pm on weekdays. Bike racks are next to the main entrance. Please do not park in the spaces marked for the café next door.

## Lockers
Day lockers in the changing rooms are free; bring your own padlock or use the keypad lockers. Lockers are cleared at 11pm every night, and anything left is kept at reception for 14 days. Permanent lockers can be rented for $8 a month.
