# Personal training

## Prices
A 30-minute personal training session costs $45 and a 60-minute session costs $75. A pack of 10 60-minute sessions costs $650. Premium members get 10% off all personal training.

## Free induction
Every new member gets a free 45-minute induction with a trainer, who will show you the equipment and write a starter programme. Book it in the app or at reception within your first 30 days.

## Choosing a trainer
Our trainers' profiles and specialities, such as strength, weight loss, injury rehab and pre and post-natal training, are in the app. You can book a free 15-minute chat with any trainer before committing.

## Cancelling a session
Personal training sessions must be cancelled at least 24 hours in advance, or the session is charged in full.
