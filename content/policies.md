# Gym rules and policies

## Towels and hygiene
Bring a towel to every session and wipe down equipment after use. Spray bottles and paper towels are at every station. Towel hire is $2.

## Refunds
Joining fees and casual passes are non-refundable. If you are charged by mistake, contact us within 30 days and we will refund the amount to your account within 5 working days.

## Lost property
Lost property is kept at reception for 14 days. Valuables such as phones, wallets and keys are kept in the safe.

## Feedback and complaints
Talk to the duty manager, use the feedback form in the app, or email hello@tidewaterfitness.example. We reply to every complaint within 2 working days.

## Privacy
We use your details only to manage your membership. CCTV footage is kept for 30 days unless it is needed for an incident.
